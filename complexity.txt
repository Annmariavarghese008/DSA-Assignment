COMPLEXITY ANALYSIS

1. Max Heap

Insertion of one element:
Time Complexity = O(log n)

Finding the maximum element:
Time Complexity = O(1)

Building the heap by inserting n elements:
Time Complexity = O(n log n)

Space Complexity:
O(n)


2. Linear Search

Finding the maximum:
Time Complexity = O(n)

Space Complexity:
O(1)
